How to interpret these numbers

Publisher-reported first decision and review timelines remain separate from calculated received → accepted intervals. The latter include author revisions and multiple review rounds. They cannot isolate editor handling or first-review time.

For each exact ISSN, discover up to 100 newest PubMed records through Europe PMC with publication dates through 10 November 2026. Screen publisher-supplied history dates. If no usable history exists, try up to two older cohorts. Retain the newest year with usable dates and show that year explicitly. Reviews, editorials, letters, corrections and retractions are excluded. DOI/PMID links and excluded records are retained.

Online publication uses an explicit electronic-publication date, never a PubMed/PMC indexing date. Mean = sum of calendar-day differences ÷ included papers. Samples cover only accepted, indexed articles with dates; they are not representative journal-wide averages. “No records” applies only to this database search, not the entire web. Publisher-domain access restrictions are recorded rather than bypassed.
